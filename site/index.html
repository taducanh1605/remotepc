Recommended browsers

Start with the latest version for your device:

  • Android: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  • Windows: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: Safari.
  • Firefox: try as an alternative if controller mapping is wrong; it is not a guaranteed fix.

Video: H.264 WebRTC support does not guarantee hardware decoding or speed. Compare decode time per frame and dropped frames at the same resolution and frame rate; some browsers do not report decode timing.

Gamepads: pair your controller with the device and test every button, trigger and stick in Standard mode. Xbox Series controller support and mapping depend on the OS, browser and device. The PWA uses the browser Gamepad API, not a raw native Xbox connection; different Chromium browsers may share the same mapping issue.

These are starting points, not a measured ranking or a guarantee. Sources: MDN WebRTC codecs, decode timing, Gamepad API, Xbox cloud gaming browsers (not a Njk benchmark).
